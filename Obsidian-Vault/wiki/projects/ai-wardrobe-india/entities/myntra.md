# Myntra

**Myntra** is an Indian fashion e-commerce platform relevant to the AI wardrobe project on three axes: AI shopping features, VTO infrastructure, and creator commerce. It is **shopping-first, not closet-first**. ^[extracted]

## Relevance

- **MyFashionGPT** — ChatGPT (later Azure OpenAI) powered conversational product discovery in the Myntra app, launched May 2023 (Microsoft collaboration Dec 2023). Shopping search, not wardrobe management. ^[extracted]
- **VTO** — PointAI's physics-based try-on powers Myntra (among Amazon/Flipkart). ^[extracted]
- **Creator Affiliate / "Ultimate Glam Clan"** — affiliates earn **8–12% by creator tier** on sales driven via social content; 6M+ UGC sign-ups; ~10% of Myntra revenue from social commerce (afaqs 2026-05-15). Requires creating 3 UGC posts in-app first — a potential *distribution* channel for a wardrobe app's "shop this look" recommendations. ^[extracted]

## Why It Matters

Myntra is both a channel partner (affiliate monetization at 8–12%) and a platform-absorption risk: it could ship closet + memory features that would compete with an independent wardrobe app. ^[inferred]
