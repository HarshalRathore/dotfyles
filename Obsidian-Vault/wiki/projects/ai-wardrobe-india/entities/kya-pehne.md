# Kya Pehne

**Kya Pehne – AI Wardrobe** (by Saransh Mittal) is an AI wardrobe + stylist app built for Indian wardrobes, treating sarees, kurtas, lehengas, sherwanis, and dupattas as first-class citizens, with occasions (office/sangeet/mehendi/haldi/puja/Diwali), a wear diary, care/repair, packing lists, Wardrobe Locator, and family profiles. ^[extracted]

## Status (as of 2026-08-15)

- iOS App Store, released **2026-08-06**, v1.0, **1 rating** — brand-new and solo-dev scale. ^[extracted]
- Free: 50 items + 10 AI stylings. Premium **₹100/mo or ₹1,000/yr** (50% intro discount from ₹200/₹2,000). ^[extracted]
- Android "coming soon" (Google Play). Site: kyapehne.in. ^[extracted]

## Why It Matters

Kya Pehne is the market research's strongest evidence of latent demand — a solo, iOS-only, ethnic-first AI wardrobe app shipped a polished, sensibly priced product in Aug 2026 with zero marketing budget — and the demonstration that **no entrenched incumbent exists**. Its constraints (iOS-only, single dev, no distribution) are exactly what a faster, Android-first, better-distributed entrant can beat. Time window: ~6–12 months before a funded clone or Kya Pehne's Android launch firms up the space. ^[extracted]
