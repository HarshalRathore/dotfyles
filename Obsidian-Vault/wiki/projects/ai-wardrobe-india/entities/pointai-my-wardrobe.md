# PointAI My Wardrobe

**PointAI "My Wardrobe"** (Noida) is a deep-tech virtual try-on (VTO) company: it builds a digital twin from a selfie and uses physics-based "Simulation AI" (claimed ~100x lower operating cost than gen-AI foundation models) for <1s try-on. It already powers Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra. ^[extracted]

## Status (as of 2026-08-15)

- ~$11M funding; Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan among backers. ^[extracted]
- Debuts Aug 2026 in India/Japan/Singapore (LiveMint 2026-07-21). ^[extracted]

## Why It Matters

PointAI is **important VTO infrastructure, not a wardrobe-memory app** — it solves "will it look good on me while I shop," not persistent catalog + taste + wear-history. For the closet-first thesis it is at once an enabler (try-on infrastructure) and a reminder that VTO is commoditizing into a platform feature. ^[inferred]
