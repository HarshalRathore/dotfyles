# Chris Best

**Chris Best** is the co-founder and CEO of [[entities/substack|Substack]] — described in Ruben Hassid's article as "a brilliant leader I met last May in SF". ^[extracted]

His July 2026 tweets (captured in the article's screenshots) define Substack's anti-slop position:
- *"We built the ability to do a @pangram scan into the @Substack app, because we're sick of slop and we don't want substack to turn into LinkedIn."* (56 replies, 115 reposts, 974 likes, 152K views at capture) ^[extracted]
- *"The problem is not people using AI, it's Claudefishing; the deception when you think you're getting a human connection but there's no one on the other end."* — coining **Claudefishing** ^[extracted]
