# Substack

**Substack** is a newsletter subscription platform. In July 2026 its CEO [[entities/chris-best|Chris Best]] announced: *"We built the ability to do a @pangram scan into the @Substack app, because we're sick of slop and we don't want substack to turn into LinkedIn."* ^[extracted]

The announcement frames Substack's anti-AI-slop stance: the concern is deception ([[concepts/ai-slop|Claudefishing]]) more than AI use per se — Best: *"The problem is not people using AI, it's Claudefishing; the deception when you think you're getting a human connection but there's no one on the other end."* ^[extracted]
