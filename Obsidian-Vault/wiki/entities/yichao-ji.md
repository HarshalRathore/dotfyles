# Yichao Ji

**Yichao 'Peak' Ji** is a co-founder of [[entities/manus|Manus]] and author of "Context Engineering for AI Agents: Lessons from Building Manus" (2025-07-18). ^[extracted]

## Background

- First decade in NLP: BERT-era fine-tuning and evaluation, weeks per iteration even on tiny models — a bitter lesson from a previous startup training models from scratch for open information extraction and semantic search; GPT-3 and Flan-T5 made those in-house models irrelevant overnight. ^[extracted]
- That history drove Manus's bet on context engineering over end-to-end agentic training, and his coinage of "Stochastic Graduate Descent" for the empirical process of context shaping. ^[extracted]
