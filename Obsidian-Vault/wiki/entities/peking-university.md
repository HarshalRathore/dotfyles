# Peking University

**Peking University** (PKU) is the academic institution listed as affiliation 1 on the Cordis paper "A Programming Paradigm for Spatiotemporal Composability." ^[extracted]

## Basic Information

- **Type:** university / research institution
- **Affiliation marker on the paper:** superscript 1 — shared by authors [[entities/yifan-shi|Yifan Shi]] and [[entities/wei-zhang|Wei Zhang]] ^[extracted]
- **Source:** the paper's author block lists "Peking University" as institution 1 and "DeepSeek-AI" as institution 2. ^[extracted]

## Description

Peking University hosts two of the three authors of the Cordis paper. Yifan Shi carries both affiliations (superscripts 1,2 — Peking University and DeepSeek-AI); Wei Zhang carries the Peking University affiliation alone. The paper itself does not otherwise describe PKU; this page exists to anchor the authorship relationship. ^[extracted]
