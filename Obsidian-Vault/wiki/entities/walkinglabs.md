# Walking Labs

**Walking Labs** is the publisher of the free online course [[misc/web-walkinglabs-github-io-learn-harness-engineering-en|Learn Harness Engineering]] — a curriculum on the engineering of AI coding agents (harnesses for [[entities/codex|Codex]], [[entities/claude-code|Claude Code]], and similar agentic tools). ^[extracted]

## What the landing page states

- Publishes the course at `walkinglabs.github.io/learn-harness-engineering/`, available in 14 languages. ^[extracted]
- Maintains the **Awesome Harness Engineering** curated list (github.com/walkinglabs/awesome-harness-engineering), cited on the course landing page as a core reference. ^[extracted]
- Runs a Discord community linked from the course site (discord.gg/XU7DQmpqk). ^[extracted]
- The course's "Try Harness" link points into the course repo's template docs (`walkinglabs/learn-harness-engineering`). ^[extracted]

## Positions

- **Harness engineering is a teachable engineering discipline** — the course's premise is that reliability of agentic coding tools comes from environment design, state management, verification, and control systems, not from model capability alone. ^[extracted]
- **A harness establishes a closed-loop working system** rather than making the model "smarter" — the core-mechanism framing on the course landing page. ^[extracted]
