# Paul D'Ambra

Paul D'Ambra is identified as a former musician and current engineering executive at PostHog. He authored the July 2026 account of PostHog's agent-assisted engineering throughput and the developer-experience systems being built around it.

## Source Contribution

The article describes the operational changes needed when agents create more pull requests: cloud dev machines, selective CI, engineering analytics, automated review, and browser-driven QA. D'Ambra reports that roughly 60% of his token spend goes to automating CI and review toil.
