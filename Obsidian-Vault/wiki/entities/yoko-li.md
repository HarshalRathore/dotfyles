# Yoko Li

**Yoko Li** (@stuffyokodraws, GitHub [ykhli](https://github.com/ykhli)) is a cartoonist, engineer, and product manager — Partner at [[entities/a16z|a16z]] investing in infrastructure & AI. Previously Product lead at HashiCorp, Founding Engineer/PM at Transposit, and Engineer at AppDynamics. Based in San Francisco; verified on X. Contact: yli@a16z.com (from her article). ^[extracted]

## Loop Engineering Work

Author of the X Article [[references/knowing-when-to-stop-loop-convergence|Knowing When to Stop: The Art of Making a Loop Converge]] (Aug 2026), an essay on when agent loops converge: the verifier defines both progress and stopping, four conditions of completion (target state, observable state, precise edits, stopping rule), editability × verifiability as the axes that determine whether a task can loop, and the economics of test-time compute (logarithmic returns, negative marginal returns past the plateau). ^[extracted]

Her Lighthouse experiment on Anthropic's loop-engineering example is a frequently-cited data point: 67% of the token bill bought zero score improvement on an impossible goal. ^[extracted]

## Visual Code Generation

The essay references her earlier writing on a similar pattern in **visual code generation**: SVG and Blender scenes as editable, inspectable representations that let agents improve an artifact across iterations rather than regenerating from scratch. ^[extracted] This positions her as one of the early voices connecting loop engineering to creative/visual domains. ^[inferred]
