# Google DeepMind

**Google DeepMind** is an AI research lab at Google that was featured at the AI Engineer World's Fair 2026 with presentations on model progression and the modality gap. ^[extracted]

## Omni Team

DeepMind's **Omni team** (led by Benoit Schillings, VP of Research) presented on: ^[extracted]

1. **Model progression framework** — the typical evolution path of language models
2. **Modality gap** — the disconnect between machine perception of audio/video and human experience
3. [[concepts/vo3|**VO3**]] — the first joint audio-visual generation model


## World Models and Agentic Coding

The keynote's DeepMind segment describes world models that combine Gemini reasoning with generative media systems for realistic video, images, and interactive simulations. It introduces the Omni family and positions Gemini 3.5 Flash as a fast model for agentic coding and long-horizon workflows. ^[extracted]

The source links this research direction to [[entities/google-antigravity|Google Antigravity]], whose harness adds sub-agents, hooks, asynchronous tasks, and multi-agent orchestration around Gemini models. ^[inferred]
