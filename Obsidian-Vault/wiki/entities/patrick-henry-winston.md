# Patrick Henry Winston

Patrick Henry Winston was an MIT professor whose **How to Speak** lecture became an annual MIT IAP tradition. MIT OpenCourseWare identifies the January 2018 recording as a non-credit teaching resource focused on improving how people present ideas in lectures, job talks, oral exams, and related settings. ^[extracted]

Winston's lecture is less a single speech recipe than a repertoire of presentation heuristics: make an empowerment promise, cycle ideas, mark transitions, distinguish the idea from nearby ideas, use boards and props deliberately, practice with listeners who lack insider context, and finish with contributions. ^[extracted]
