# Infoproducts GTM

Chapter 3 of the GTM agent machine focuses on creators who sell courses, templates, cohorts, or memberships. The core job is owning the audience you sell to instead of renting it, and keeping members past the second month. This chapter is a concrete application of the [[concepts/loop-engineering-product-vs-research-loops|loop engineering]] philosophy to the creator/membership domain.

## Key Ideas

### Owned Email

- **Trigger**: A post that performed well. The agent identifies which post deserves a lead magnet before the human does by reading the platform's native numbers.^[extracted]
- **Output**: From that post, the agent builds:
  - A short guide (lead magnet)
  - Landing page copy for the guide
  - A welcome sequence behind it
- **Monetization from day one**: The sequence carries a small offer right away — a low-ticket product in the same format as the guide — because "a subscriber who bought once is a different list than a subscriber who only read."^[extracted]
- **Referral mechanism**: From the first email, every subscriber gets a referral link and a reason to use it, so the list grows on its own readers.
- **Reporting**: Each week, a report of what came in from where.^[extracted]
- **Approval gate**: The guide, landing page, and sequence all wait for human read before anything goes live under your name.^[extracted]
- **Recommended first workflow**: For creators, start with owned email because "everything else in that chapter fills a list that does not exist yet."^[extracted] This aligns with the general principle that workflows should be built one at a time, as described in [[concepts/agent-vs-workflow|agent vs workflow]].

### Content Batch from Receipts

- **Process**: Every week starts with the agent reading what already worked — top posts, transcripts, replies with the most engagement — the way an editor would read them to find the angles that earned attention.
- **Output**: Agent drafts the week's batch for each platform in your voice, flags the strongest one and explains why, and adds a poll for the newsletter so readers choose the next topic.
- **Receipt-driven iteration**: The next batch starts from a receipt (actual performance data), not a guess.^[extracted]
- **Approval gate**: Nothing publishes on its own. Every draft waits for edit and approval. Corrections become the standing rules for the next batch.^[extracted]

### Borrowed Rooms

- **Concept**: The audience you need already exists in rooms someone else built — newsletters your readers open, podcasts they follow, small creators posting in your niche, community threads where they ask questions.^[extracted]
- **Monthly process**: Agent studies your own audience's behavior: what they share, who they cite, which small accounts they engage with.
- **Room sheet** with three kinds of rows:
  1. **Small creators** who could make short videos about the product for a fixed fee plus a bonus that only pays on verified views
  2. **Newsletters and podcasts** that sell placements to their readers
  3. **Weekly conversations** in your niche you can join with a real contribution^[extracted]
- **Each row comes with a drafted first message**, every message waits for your approval.^[extracted]
- **Priority**: "Borrowed rooms before bought reach."^[extracted]

### Member Retention

- **Core problem**: Paid communities lose members in the second month, almost always because "the member never did the first thing."^[extracted]
- **Post-checkout sequence** (agent runs small actions you only see when one fails):
  - Welcome within the hour with one first action to take
  - Check that a small win happened inside two days
  - Weekly ritual on a fixed day so the week has one thing to show up for
  - Inactivity nudge after a week and again after two
  - At-risk list posted to your channel with win-back drafts attached^[extracted]
- **Approval gate**: Win-back drafts wait for your approval.^[extracted]
- **Quarterly option setting**: A quarterly option at signup drops the monthly cancel decision from 12 per year to 4 per year.^[extracted]
