# AI Virtual Try-On Landscape

Virtual try-on (VTO) is a clear e-commerce product-market fit (see [[concepts/virtual-try-on|Virtual Try-On]]), and by 2026 it is rapidly becoming a platform feature. The India-specific landscape from the 2026-08 market research: ^[extracted]

## Players

- **[[projects/ai-wardrobe-india/entities/pointai-my-wardrobe|PointAI "My Wardrobe"]]** (Noida) — deep-tech VTO: digital twin from a selfie, physics-based "Simulation AI" claiming ~100x lower operating cost than gen-AI foundation models, <1s try-on; already powers Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra; ~$11M funding with Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan among backers; debuts Aug 2026 in India/Japan/Singapore. ^[extracted]
- **Google Virtual Apparel Try-On (India)** — launched Dec 2025; "Try it on" on supported listings (tops/bottoms/dresses/jackets/shoes); **ethnic wear not mentioned**. ^[extracted]
- **Myntra MyFashionGPT** — ChatGPT (later Azure OpenAI) conversational product discovery since May 2023 — shopping search, not wardrobe management. ^[extracted]
- **Independent Indian/founder-led VTO + closet utilities** — Aina, Only2U, Figur, fit. (0–9 ratings each, 2026), StyleDotMe (site down / dormant), THINGS (AI-native shopping companion with "ME" VTO), Anokhi Reet (occasion-wear rental). ^[extracted]
- **Global context** — Amazon Magic Fit / Fit Review, Walmart/Zeekit, Dress-X, Letsy as retailer/consumer VTO; Alta and Fits bundle VTO into US/EU closet apps. ^[extracted]

## Key Pattern: Shop-First, Not Closet-First

The recurring observation is that every VTO player answers "will it look good on me while I shop" — none keep a persistent catalog + taste + wear-history across occasions. The closet-first memory layer remains unclaimed, and the wardrobe-catalog app's fragility is that platforms could absorb VTO as a feature. ^[extracted]
