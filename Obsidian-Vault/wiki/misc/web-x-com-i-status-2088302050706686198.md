## Distilled Framework

The accessible post description identifies four priority areas:

- **Building and deploying AI applications** — including evaluation, measurement, error analysis, and governance for probabilistic systems.
- **Software-engineering fundamentals** — architecture, security, scalability, maintainability, and cost trade-offs remain essential.
- **Coding-agent use** — context management, planning, specification, orchestration, verification, and safe deployment. ^[inferred]
- **Shaping the build** — product judgment, business understanding, and ownership of what gets built. ^[inferred]

Continuous learning is presented as necessary because tools and practices evolve quickly. ^[inferred]
